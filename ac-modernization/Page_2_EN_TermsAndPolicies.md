## Re-using content on this site

CHIN releases and updates technical documentation and information pertaining to the modernization of Artefacts Canada as regularly as it can. The working documents found on this site primarily pertain to scope, design and specifications, testing procedures, as well as methodologies, and include diagrams, illustrations, and examples. 

We encourage you to re-use the content on this site, but ask that you review the license attributed to it and follow applicable guidelines.
